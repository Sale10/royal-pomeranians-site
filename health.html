The Royal Seal of Health

Our Unwavering Commitment

At Royal Pomeranians, we don't just say our puppies are vet-checked and healthy; we guarantee it with a comprehensive policy built on over thirty years of understanding breed health and standard veterinary care. We work tirelessly with specialized canine reproductive veterinarians to ensure every litter starts with impeccable health foundations.

What We Guarantee:

  • Sound Genetic Background: Parents are screened for common genetic disorders within the Pomeranian breed (including, but not limited to, Patellas, Hips, Eyes, and heart evaluations), ensuring we are only breeding healthy lines.
  • Sound and Health on Delivery: We guarantee that every puppy is in good health at the time of delivery. They have received multiple veterinary exams and a final certification of wellness.
  • Up-to-Date Medical Care: Every Royal Pomeranian puppy leaves with a full medical history, including multiple preventative dewormings and their initial series of essential vaccinations.
